About Fern & Fig

A small shop with a simple goal: plants that live.

Our mission

We want every plant that leaves our shop to still be alive a year later. That means selling plants that suit the homes our customers actually have, being honest about how much care each one needs, and answering questions long after the sale.

Our story

Fern & Fig began in 2021 as a folding table at a Saturday farmers market, selling cuttings from a single overgrown pothos. The cuttings sold out every week, and the questions never stopped: how much light, how often to water, why are the leaves turning yellow?

In 2024 we moved into a storefront in NoDa with room for a repotting bench and a care desk. We still grow many of our plants from cuttings, and we still write every care card by hand.

What we believe

Right plant, right place

We will talk you out of a plant if your space can't support it.

Grown, not shipped

Most of our stock is propagated locally, not trucked across the country.

Help after the sale

Bring in a struggling plant any time for a free check-up.
